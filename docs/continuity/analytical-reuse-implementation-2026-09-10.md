- Supported native and wrapped calls retain full permitted query/parameter inputs as hashed artifacts. Missing, redacted or undelivered bytes stay labelled. Artifact acceptance checks content hashes and exact event membership, including cross-session deduplication; they do not prove the analytical reasoning correct.
- Classification processes the earliest unprocessed event page without skipping omitted history. Candidate relevance is evaluated over the allowed repository/non-code scope before prompt limits, including older closed work; confirmed summaries have a separate budget from proposals. Retrieval is lexical and bounded, with omissions explicit.
