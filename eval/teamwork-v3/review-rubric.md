# Independent PM continuation review

Judge the delivered answer, report and work state against the actual evidence. Source roles distinguish canonical released ground truth, artifacts present at the recovery checkpoint, artifacts present at delivery, and independent supplier observations. Ground truth being visible to a reviewer does not prove the task agent recovered or read it. Exact recovery of an incorrect predecessor claim does not make the claim correct. A saved/native-stable recommendation is not human approval; use explicit decision-authority evidence. Conversely, available predecessor artifacts can support legitimate inherited plans and must not be ignored merely because they came through a memory system.

Score each PM dimension 0–4 (0 absent/contradictory; 1 weak; 2 adequate; 3 strong; 4 unusually complete and precise), with source/answer passages:

1. Problem framing and population: actual user problem, decision owner, applicable market/window and objective.
2. Evidence synthesis: combines research and measurements; handles the tracking correction; does not infer causality from nonrandom comparisons.
3. Alternatives and opportunity cost: compares feasible choices, including deferral, with capacity and downside.
4. Uncertainty and information value: names decision-changing assumptions and proposes a discriminating next observation rather than generic more research.
5. Executable decision: bounded scope, owner, resources, success/stop thresholds and revisit time.
6. Updating and consequences: applies later evidence, identifies affected earlier plans, preserves still-valid history and avoids treating proposals as approval.

Require a separate critical-error list: fabricated customer fact; invalid denominator/causal assertion driving the recommendation; exceeded hard budget; unauthorized proposal treated as accepted; continuing above a mandatory stop threshold without acknowledging/handling it; stale result applied to the wrong population. A critical error prevents a decision-quality pass regardless of polished prose. A reviewer cannot assign a critical error solely because it prefers another feasible product choice.


Assess these five factual categories independently: numeric_result, scope_and_denominator, decision_authority, predecessor_attribution, operation_state. Each verdict is supported, contradicted, unclear, not_stated, or not_applicable, with a rationale and literal answer/source excerpts. Interpret complete claims, including negations, scope and uncertainty; no canonical prose string or predetermined investment choice is required. Not_stated concerns absence of a substantive claim; not_applicable requires a stage-based explanation. Unavailable corroboration is not automatically contradiction. These are model judgments whose evidence binding is checked mechanically, not independently proven semantic labels.

Two fresh independent reviews are required. Differences in any dimension score, factual verdict, or critical error trigger a third fresh adjudicator. Keep all original judgments and disagreements; use the adjudicator only to resolve that case under this rubric. There is no numerical overall pass threshold or pooled leaderboard. Missing, late, malformed or identity-exposed reviews remain not_evaluated without retries. Report calibration and same-model limitations; model review is not human review.

The local self-check validates schema and literal excerpt binding only. It supplies no hidden expected facts, product identities or quality feedback. Schema/citation repairs may occur within the original reviewer session and deadline. Real identity concerns must remain visible. Native retrieval/use, artifact recovery and task timing are assessed separately.
